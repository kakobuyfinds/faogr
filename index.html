A practical starting point

How to use an iTaoBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet helps you organize a decision. The useful record is the product plus its exact option, measurements, source and cost assumptions. Keep those details together so a similar image does not lead you to compare different items.

01 / IDENTIFY

Match the actual listing

Open the source record and compare its item number, images and description. Record the seller’s original marketplace URL when available. A catalog record number is not necessarily a Taobao or Weidian product ID.

02 / COMPARE

Choose an exact variant

Note color, size and package contents. For clothing, measure a garment you own and compare chest width, length and sleeves using the seller’s method. For footwear, check the stated size system and length chart.

03 / VERIFY

Resolve the unknowns

Confirm whether your purchasing service accepts the source link and product type. Review current fees, return conditions and shipping options. If inspection is available, check photographs of your actual item before approving dispatch.

iTaoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list used to organize finds, prices and listing references for research. This page features records from HipobuyFind and does not provide an official iTaoBuy catalog or checkout.

Where do the two main buttons go?

Both the “iTaoBuy spreadsheet” button and the “Buy it now” button open https://hipobuyfind.com. They do not place an order. Individual product links open the matching record on the same external catalog.

Do these prices include shipping?

No. Displayed CNY values are source catalog item prices from a dated snapshot. Shipping, service charges, exchange costs, taxes and variant price differences may be additional. Recheck the chosen option before relying on a price.

Are these product photos QC evidence?

No. Catalog images are listing images. They do not establish the condition, measurements or authenticity of an item you may receive. Request order-specific evidence when the purchasing service offers it.

Can I use these records directly with iTaoBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified. Open the catalog record, identify the underlying seller listing and check whether iTaoBuy currently supports that source, item and destination. Do not treat the HipobuyFind record URL as a confirmed agent purchase link.

Is this an Excel download or a live stock feed?

This is a browser-based directory with 12 embedded records. It does not download an Excel workbook or automatically synchronize inventory. The displayed snapshot remains unchanged until the page is updated.
